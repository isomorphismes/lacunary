# Lacunary

Experimental complex-analysis and Riemann-surface work that does not belong in the ordinary meromorphic random-holomorphic explorer.

This repository is the home for:

- lasso / deformed-domain experiments;
- overlapping convergence-disc and reveal experiments;
- bounded-domain reproducing-kernel perturbations, including the historical unit-disc Bergman construction;
- analytic continuation along paths;
- branch, sheet, monodromy, and Riemann-surface experiments;
- related historical prototypes migrated out of `isomorphismes/analytic-continuation`.

`isomorphismes/analytic-continuation` remains focused on the Wegert-style meromorphic plane with explicit zeros and poles plus a continuously varying holomorphic/nonvanishing factor. Wegert owns the reusable rendering preferences and ordinary phase-portrait behavior.

Historical code copied here should keep its provenance and should not be mistaken for the final architecture.

The recovered Bergman construction and its whole-plane limitation are recorded in [`archive/bergman-disc/README.md`](archive/bergman-disc/README.md). It is a legitimate bounded-disc extremal, not the hidden perturbation space of the current Analytic Continuation app.
